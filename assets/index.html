Both sensors are real, sharing one I2C0 bus safely via the official embassy shared-bus pattern (BME280 at address 0x76, MPU6050 at 0x68) - both proven to keep working correctly with Wi-Fi active, unlike the DHT22's single-wire protocol tried earlier. MPU6050's Z-axis feeds a real on-device FFT (256-sample window, ~1.28s), whose result now drives the same LED/buzzer/motor alarm as temperature and humidity.
